Practice Room recorded instruments

Piano: Salamander Grand Piano, Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Changes: selected three velocity layers and minor-third pitch intervals;
converted recordings to mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, trimmed/levelled/faded,
limited decay to seven seconds, and mapped into a SoundFont instrument.
This is a subset of the recordings, not the original SFZ implementation.
Full license: salamander.txt.

Bass: Karoryfer Black and Blue Basses, CC0 1.0.
https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-black-and-blue-basses
Selected the fingered black hollowbody bass, four velocity layers.
Full license: karoryfer-bass.txt.

Guitar: Karoryfer Black and Green Guitars, CC0 1.0.
https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-black-and-green-guitars
Selected the green hollowbody guitar, three velocity layers.
Full license: karoryfer-guitar.txt.

Bass/guitar changes: selected every third semitone, converted to mono
44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, trimmed/levelled/faded, limited decay to five seconds,
and mapped into SoundFont instruments. Source URLs and SHA-256 checksums,
trim points, gains and mappings are in src/audio/assets/band-manifest.json.

Drums and woodblock: Kount's Memory Bank Drum Pack, from the owner's library.
These recordings retain the pack's license; no CC0, CC BY or Apache license
is granted for them. Selected hits are trimmed/levelled/faded and converted
to mono 44.1 kHz Ogg; the woodblock is mono 48 kHz PCM at original level.

The underlying General MIDI bank is alphaTab's SONiVOX SoundFont,
Apache-2.0. See the SONiVOX notice distributed alongside the bank.
